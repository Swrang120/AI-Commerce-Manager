ABOUT E-COMMERCE SHOPING

A complete shopping experience, built for mobile and desktop.

E-commerce Shoping is a web-first shopping platform focused on a simple journey from discovery to checkout. Customers can browse active products, search by keyword, explore categories, save favourites, manage their cart, maintain a delivery profile and check order information from the same storefront.

The customer experience is responsive: the same core options are available on Android phones, tablets and desktop screens, while the layout adapts automatically to the available space.
